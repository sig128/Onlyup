How ONLY UP works — nothing hidden

Every rule here is enforced by the contract, not by us. The code is open source, and below you can play with a simulator that runs the contract's real fee math to see what you'd earn in different scenarios.

Price = SOL in the safe ÷ tokens

The contract holds a safe of SOL. One token is worth the SOL in the safe divided by how many tokens exist. Fees only ever add SOL to the safe, so that number can only grow. The contract cancels any trade that would make it drop.

What happens on every trade

↑

You buy

Your SOL goes into the safe. The contract mints fresh tokens to you at the current price plus a 2.5% fee. That fee stays in the safe.

↓

You sell

The contract burns your tokens and pays you the current price minus a 3% fee, from the safe. Part of that fee stays behind and lifts the price. Selling can never be paused.

✓

The price ticks up

Because the fee stayed in the safe, there's now a little more SOL per token — for everyone holding, instantly. Nothing to claim. Then the contract checks the price didn't fall.

Every 3% fee splits:
2.5% → holders (into the safe)
0.5% creator

Everything the contract can and can't do

✓ What anyone can do

  • Buy — pay SOL, get tokens at the price + fee.
  • Sell — always available, never pausable, fixed fee.
  • Donate — add SOL to the safe for everyone, minting nothing.
  • Claim creator fees — anyone can trigger it, but the SOL only ever goes to the creator's set wallet.

✕ What nobody can do — not even us

  • Take SOL out of the safe (the only payout is to sellers).
  • Mint tokens for free — new tokens only come from a paid buy.
  • Change the fees after launch. They're fixed in the code.
  • Freeze your wallet or block your sell. There's no freeze authority.
  • Make the price go down — every trade re-checks it.

The guards, in plain words

Price can't fall. Every buy and sell cross-checks the new price against the old one and reverts if it's lower. No rounding can sneak it down — rounding always favours the safe.

No sandwiches. The sell fee is never below the buy fee, and a buy can lift the price by at most the buy fee — so a bot buying right before you and selling after always loses. Proven across 50,000 random tests.

The safe always covers it. After every move, the contract re-checks the safe holds at least the whole reserve plus unclaimed creator fees. Sellers can always be paid.

The seed is locked forever. The starting SOL and starting tokens are locked at launch and can never be moved, so the safe can't be drained to zero.

The contract owns the mint. It's the only thing that can create tokens, and only against a paid buy. The deployer hands this over at launch and can't take it back.

The admin is near-powerless. It can only pause buying (never selling), point the creator fee at a new wallet, or renounce. Then it can touch nothing at all.

The honest trade-off

A normal memecoin can 100x — because it can also go to zero and dump on whoever's left. ONLY UP can't do that to you, which means it also won't 100x for you overnight. You earn from fees and steady growth instead, and your downside is capped at the fee you paid. That's the whole deal: you can have a chart that never goes down, or a fast 100x — not both. The simulator shows real scenarios, not a promise, so you can decide if that trade is for you.
