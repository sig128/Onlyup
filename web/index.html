Questions

Can it really never go down?

In SOL, yes. The contract cancels any trade that would lower the price. In dollars it moves with the price of SOL.

Can I sell whenever I want?

Yes. Sell to the contract any time, at the current price, minus the fixed 3% fee. Nobody can pause selling.

How do holders earn?

From every trade. 2.5% of each buy and sell stays in the safe and lifts the price the instant it lands. Your share grows with the price. There's nothing to claim.

Can a bot sandwich my buy?

No. A buy lifts the price by at most the buy fee, and the sell fee is never lower, so buying right before you and selling right after always loses money. It's proven in the contract's tests.

Why not a 100x?

A fast 100x is paid for by people who buy late and then get dumped on. ONLY UP doesn't let anyone get dumped on, so it grows step by step instead.
