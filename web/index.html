Because it is the shop. It creates new tokens only when someone pays SOL into the safe at the current price, and destroys them when someone sells. Nobody can mint for free, not even us.

Questions

Can the price go down?

The price can — it trades up and down like any coin. What can't go down is the floor: the SOL you can always redeem per token only ever rises.

Can I sell whenever I want?

Yes. Sell any time, at the better of the price or the floor, minus a 1% fee. Nobody can pause selling.

How do I make money?

Two ways: the price can climb like any coin, and the floor underneath rises with every buy. 40% of each buy funds that floor, instantly, for everyone holding.

Can a bot sandwich my buy?

No. Nobody can buy below the floor and redeem for a profit, so buying right before you and selling right after always loses money. It's proven in the contract's tests.

Can it still 100x?

Yes — it trades on a real curve, so the upside is real. The difference is the floor underneath: your downside is capped and only rises.
